Admin Control Panel Guide

What every menu item under the Admin Control Panel is for, with a screenshot of each — and, for Website Management, how to create or amend the site content it controls.

Source note: Every screenshot and every factual claim on this page is drawn from the application's actual code, captured directly from the running app by scripts/screenshots/admin-menu-tour.spec.ts against the isolated test database — run npm run screenshots:admin-menu to regenerate them, then npm run guide:admin-menu-pdf to re-export this page as admin-control-panel-guide.pdf.

Logged in as: these screenshots use an ordinary admin account, not a super admin. Three controls are hidden for a plain admin and are called out where relevant: the Business email field on Content Control, the Delete all button on Audit Log, and the on/off toggle on Test Mode.

Admin Control Panel (Dashboard)

1

Daily Overview

The landing page after logging in as an admin — a “Daily Overview” for a selected date (change it with the date picker at the top). Shows new online bookings awaiting confirmation, bookings at risk because a dog’s vaccinations are lapsing, three occupancy summary cards (Home Boarding, Day Care, and other service types), and a to-do list filterable by staff assignee. Everything here links through to the relevant screen — nothing is created or edited on this page itself.

Customers & Dogs

2

Customers

Search customers by name or email and open any result to see their full detail page. New customer records aren’t created from this list — they come from a customer registering themselves at /register, or an admin adding one over the phone from the Bookings screen (see the separate Create Account & Log In guide for both of those journeys).

3

Dogs

Every dog on file, searchable, with a link through to that dog’s bookings and its owner’s customer record, and a delete option. Dogs are added from the customer portal or the admin’s phone-booking screen, not from this list.

Calendar & Occupancy

4

Calendar

A month grid of everything scheduled — bookings and van runs — click any day to see its entries, or click through to a specific booking or run. Use Prev/Next to move between months. Nothing is created here directly.

5

Occupancy › Home Boarding

Every kennel/accommodation unit against each day of the selected month, with the month’s overall occupancy percentage in the header — a quick way to see which nights are full and which units are free. Capacity itself (how many units exist) is managed on Pricing & Capacity, not here.

6

Occupancy › Day Care

The same day-by-day occupancy view as Home Boarding, scoped to Day Care bookings instead of kennel units.

Bookings & Waitlist

7

Bookings

Every booking, filterable, with a “Create manual booking” button for taking a booking over the phone. That flow — including adding a new customer partway through it — is covered step by step in the separate Create Account & Log In guide, since it’s as much a customer-creation journey as a booking one.

8

Waitlist

Two things live here: bookings needing attention because a dog’s vaccination has lapsed, and waitlist groups (by service and date) with an “Offer to next in line” button to notify whoever’s waiting next. Customers join the waitlist themselves — there’s no admin-authored “add to waitlist” form.

Website Management

Everything under here edits what the public website shows, or what customers are asked to agree to — the one part of the Admin Control Panel covered in extra depth below, since “how do I change X on the site” is the most common admin question.

9

Services We Offer — list

Every bookable service — name, whether it’s active, how it’s paid for, its price, and its URL slug. This is also where a service is switched active/inactive or deleted.

10

Add service — empty form

To create a service: click “Add service” on the list above. This opens a blank form.

11

Add service — filled in

The form filled in with an example service, ready to submit via “Create service”. Pricing model controls how the price is interpreted (per night, per day, or per session); Payment timing controls whether the customer pays a deposit now and the balance later, the whole amount upfront, or is invoiced afterwards. To amend an existing service, click “Edit” next to its row on the list instead — it’s the same form, pre-filled with the current values, with a “Save changes” button in place of “Create service”.

12

Media Control

One page with everything media-related: gallery categories, an “Add media” form, and the full media library below it. Existing items can be edited (via a separate small form) or deleted directly from the library grid.

13

Add media — filled in

To add media: choose a file, fill in a caption and alt text, and submit via “Add media”, as shown here. Type (Image/Video/Embed) auto-corrects to match whatever file is chosen, and Used on decides whether it appears in the public gallery, as a hero image, on a specific service’s page, or on the About page.

14

Content Control — overview

A long settings page — around fifteen independent sections, each with its own Save button — covering everything from the business’s contact details to opening hours, FAQs, testimonials, and VAT settings. Only the top of the page is shown here (a full-page capture of all fifteen sections would be one unreadably long image); the next screenshot drills into one section as a worked example.

15

Content Control — Business details

To amend any section on this page: edit its fields and click that section’s own Save button, as shown here for “Business details” — which feeds the site header/footer, the contact page, and the footer of every outgoing email. Every section works the same way. One exception: the Business email section further down is hidden from a plain admin and only editable by a super admin.

16

Terms and Conditions — overview

The legally-binding agreement customers must sign before booking — distinct from the plain-text “Terms & Conditions” copy on the public /legal/terms page, which is edited from Content Control instead. This page shows the currently active version and a full version history.

17

Publish a new version — file selected

To publish a new version: choose a PDF under “Publish a new version” — this enables the “Publish version N” button.

18

Publish confirmation — read before clicking

Clicking “Publish version N” opens this confirmation before anything happens. Publishing is irreversible: every customer — including anyone who already signed an earlier version — is immediately asked to sign the new one before their next booking, and the old version can never be restored or edited, only superseded by publishing another.

Pricing & Capacity

19

Pricing & Capacity

Deposit/balance settings and capacity limits, the list of accommodation units (kennels), and blocked dates — all editable inline on this one page, except an existing accommodation unit’s own details, which open on a separate small page via its “Edit” link.

Van Runs

20

Van Runs

Upcoming delivery/collection runs, a “Create van run” button, and a “Service area” form controlling which postcodes van runs are offered to. A run can only be deleted while it has no stops on it yet.

Reviews

21

Reviews

Customer-submitted reviews awaiting a decision, with Approve/Reject buttons, plus a log of recent decisions below. Reviews are never admin-authored — only moderated.

Reports & Accounting

22

Reports

Read-only figures: revenue so far this month, a CSV export, a breakdown of revenue by service, and a forecast for the coming week.

23

Accounting

The full payment ledger for a VAT period (Prev/Next to move between periods), with Net/VAT/Gross totals and filters by customer, booking number, payment status, and service. Read-only other than the filters.

Sent Emails & Email Templates

24

Sent Emails

A log of every email the application has sent, for checking whether a particular customer notification actually went out (and why, if it failed) — a link to the Resend dashboard is provided for deeper delivery details. Nothing is created here.

25

Email Templates

The handful of emails with editable subject/body text (payment receipts and booking-confirmation invoices), edited in place with merge-field hints shown alongside each editor.

Audit Log

26

Audit Log

A filterable record of who did what — admin actions and significant customer self-service actions alike. A super admin additionally has a “Delete all” button here, which a plain admin (as shown) doesn’t see.

Test Mode

27

Test Mode

A safety switch for creating test data without accidentally emailing real customers — while on, every new dog’s vaccination/Meet & Greet checks are bypassed and outgoing email is redirected to the business address. The toggle itself is super-admin-only; a plain admin sees the description but not the control, as shown here.
