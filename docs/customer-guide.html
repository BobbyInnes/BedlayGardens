Create Account & Log In (Customer)

A new customer registering for the first time, logging back in, and the error states they might see along the way.

1

Home page

The home page — the starting point for a new customer, with “Log in” and “Book Now” in the top navigation.

2

Registration form — empty

The registration form at /register, before anything has been filled in.

3

Registration form — filled

The same form filled in with test data, ready to submit.

4

Confirmation after submitting

Submitting creates the account and signs the customer in automatically — there is no separate “thank you” page, they land straight on their dashboard.

5

Login page

The login page at /login, with the “Password” tab selected by default (an “Email link” option is also available).

6

Login form — filled

The login form filled in with the account’s email and password.

7

Dashboard after login

The customer dashboard after a successful login.

8

Error — wrong password

Logging in with the correct email but an incorrect password shows “Invalid email or password.” — the same message either way, so a wrong guess never reveals whether an email is registered.

9

Error — email already registered

Trying to register again with an email that already has an account shows “An account with this email already exists.”

10

Error — empty required fields

Submitting the registration form with the required fields left blank shows a message under each one that needs attention.

Creating a Customer (Admin)

How an admin adds a new customer while taking a booking over the phone, from /admin/bookings/new.

1

New booking — customer search

The “Create manual booking” screen starts on the customer search step, for finding an existing customer by name or email.

2

New customer form — empty

Clicking “New phone customer” reveals the new-customer form for someone who isn’t in the system yet.

3

New customer form — filled

The form filled in with valid data, ready to submit.

4

Customer created

Submitting creates the customer — the panel collapses to a summary and the “2. Dogs” step appears below it, ready to continue building the booking.

5

Error — email already registered

Starting a second, separate customer with the email just used above shows “A customer with that email already exists — search for them instead.”

6

Error — missing email

Email isn’t part of the form’s client-side required-field check, so an empty email only surfaces “Enter a valid email address” once submitted.
